# ACME Corporation - 2024 Financial & Operational Highlights

## Overview
ACME Corporation is a leading provider of enterprise data analytics and cloud workflow solutions. This report summarizes the financial results for the first half of fiscal year 2024.

## Q1 2024 Financial Performance
- Total Revenue: $120.0 million
- Subscription Revenue: $96.0 million (80% of total)
- Professional Services Revenue: $24.0 million
- Operating Expenses: $75.0 million
- Research & Development (R&D): $30.0 million
- Sales & Marketing (S&M): $32.0 million
- General & Administrative (G&A): $13.0 million
- Net Income: $45.0 million
- Headcount at end of Q1: 1,250 full-time employees

## Q2 2024 Financial Performance
- Total Revenue: $150.0 million
- Subscription Revenue: $127.5 million (85% of total)
- Professional Services Revenue: $22.5 million
- Operating Expenses: $82.5 million
- Research & Development (R&D): $35.0 million
- Sales & Marketing (S&M): $34.5 million
- General & Administrative (G&A): $13.0 million
- Net Income: $67.5 million
- Headcount at end of Q2: 1,400 full-time employees

## Geographic Performance
- North America accounted for 62% of consolidated H1 revenue.
- EMEA (Europe, Middle East, Africa) contributed 26%.
- APAC (Asia-Pacific) contributed 12%.

## Strategic Highlights
In May 2024, ACME completed the acquisition of DataForge AI for $45 million in cash, expanding its natural language processing capabilities.
The company also launched CloudStream v3, reducing processing latencies by 40%.
