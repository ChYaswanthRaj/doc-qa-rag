# ACME Corporation - Employee Business Travel & Expense Policy (v3.2)

## 1. Scope and Objective
This policy outlines the principles and reimbursement procedures for business-related travel and expenses incurred by ACME Corporation employees.

## 2. Flight & Rail Booking Guidelines
- All domestic flights must be booked in Economy Class at least 14 days in advance through the corporate travel portal (Concur).
- International flights exceeding 7 hours in flight duration qualify for Premium Economy upon written approval by the Department VP.
- Business Class is only permitted for intercontinental flights exceeding 10 hours with approval from the Chief Financial Officer (CFO).

## 3. Lodging and Hotel Rates
- Standard hotel room rates must not exceed $220 per night in Tier 1 cities (New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo) and $160 per night in all other locations, excluding taxes.
- Incidental expenses such as hotel gym access or minibar items are non-reimbursable.

## 4. Daily Meals & Incidentals (Per Diem)
- The daily maximum meal allowance is $75 per day for domestic travel.
  - Breakfast: up to $15
  - Lunch: up to $25
  - Dinner: up to $35
- For international travel, the daily maximum meal allowance is $100 per day.
- Itemized receipts are mandatory for any individual meal expense exceeding $25. Alcoholic beverages are non-reimbursable unless part of an authorized client dinner.

## 5. Ground Transportation & Mileage
- Personal vehicle business mileage is reimbursed at $0.67 per mile.
- Employees are encouraged to use rideshare services (standard tier Uber/Lyft) or public transit for airport transfers.
